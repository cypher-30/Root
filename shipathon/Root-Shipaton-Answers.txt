## Inspiration

We have always been drawn to cultures and languages. And we kept meeting the same person in different forms: someone who can name every dish at a family gathering, who laughs at the right moment in a story, who recognises their grandmother's greeting the second they hear it, but who can't answer back in their own indigenous language.

Often it isn't because they didn't care. It's because they grew up away from the place where that language lives every day: in a city, in another country, in a school that taught in English. The language didn't disappear. It just got quieter with every mile.

The big language apps rarely teach these languages, and when they do, they are built for tourists: order a coffee, find the hotel. A heritage learner wants something else. They want to greet an elder properly. To understand the proverb at a wedding. To say *karibu* and mean it.

So we built **Root**, around one belief:

> **Distance from home shouldn't become distance from your language.**

## What it does

Root is a calm Android app for reconnecting with a heritage language, starting with **Dholuo, Shona, Swahili and Amharic**. It is a session, not a destination: open it, bring one word back to mind, and leave a little closer to home.

- **Practice** opens straight onto a recall card. *"Take a breath. The word is somewhere in you."* Reveal it, rate yourself honestly (Missed, Close or Got it), and watch a root grow, but only for the words you truly remembered. No streaks, no leaderboards, no guilt.
- **Learn** gives every language a gentle path of guided conversations, pattern workshops and stories. Nothing is locked, and a lesson you finish stays finished.
- **Explore** is language as it's lived: everyday **Situations** (greetings, meeting people, the market, getting around), **Stories & culture** (welcoming new neighbours in Mombasa, finding the way in Addis Ababa, why the Ethiopian clock starts at dawn), and **My notebook** for the words worth keeping.
- **Your family's words.** Add a phrase your family actually uses, record a reference with their permission, then record yourself and compare. Your recordings stay on your phone.
- **Real voices only.** Where a genuine recording exists you hear it, with the speaker credited by name. Where one doesn't, Root says so. We never fake a voice with text-to-speech.
- Works offline with no account, in warm-paper light and warm-charcoal dark themes, with a home-screen widget and a shareable card to teach someone one word.

## How we built it

Root is fully native: **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose** with Material 3. A versioned **Room** database keeps everything on the device, including recall history, lesson attempts, saved phrases and recordings, with tested migrations so updates keep a learner's progress. WorkManager downloads content packs that are size-bounded, hash-verified and only activated once complete. Glance powers the widget.

Content runs through a **Python toolchain** that validates versioned lesson packs and tracks where every line came from. Each starter phrase (39 to 40 per language) was checked against a public reference such as Wikivoyage, Omniglot, Wiktionary or the public-domain FSI Amharic course, and the sources ship inside the app.

**RevenueCat** powers Root Premium, and we designed it around who our learners are. Most heritage learners want *one* language: the one their family speaks. So Premium is a one-time unlock for a single language (US$4.99) or for every language (US$9.99), and never a subscription. It opens two extra phrase sets per language, like **Heart words** (*I love you*, *long time no see*, *get well soon*) and **Keep talking** (*please say that again*, *speak slowly*), the words you reach for with family. Each language has its own entitlement (`premium_<language>`), and `premium` is the all-languages bundle. Purchases run through RevenueCat's Test Store for this showcase, entitlements are cached for offline use, restore is built in, and a language is only sold once it has premium sets in the app.

The design is deliberately quiet. Source Serif 4 carries the words and Inter the interface. One hand-drawn root path becomes the logo, the launch animation and your progress, and an original startup sound is scored to that animation.

## Challenges we ran into

- **Honesty over volume.** It would have been easy to fill the app with machine translations and synthetic voices. We refused. Finding recordings we could legally use, that matched the exact word, was slow: today six phrases play genuine Lingua Libre recordings, each credited to its volunteer speaker.
- **Earning money without betraying the mission.** We would not put practice behind a paywall. Every starter set stays free. Premium adds more sets on top, and nothing is sold before it exists in the app.
- **Progress you can trust.** At one point, reopening a finished lesson quietly reset it. We rebuilt completion as durable history, so reviewing never erases what you achieved.
- **Four languages, one switch.** Changing language had to change Practice, Learn and Explore together, without losing anything in the language you left behind.

## Accomplishments that we're proud of

- A complete, working app in four African languages: over 220 source-checked phrases, 8 Learn units with 24 lessons, 8 short stories and 8 sourced culture notes.
- A monetisation model that respects the people it is for.
- A design that lets the language lead: no confetti, no pressure, just the word.
- Over 350 automated tests across the app, real devices and the content pipeline.

## What we learned

- The technology is the easy part of a language app. Trust is the hard part: every source, recording and credit matters.
- Heritage learners don't need to be pushed. They need a doorway, and a reason to come back.
- Monetisation design is product design. Choosing per-language pricing reshaped our entitlements, our content model and our paywall.

## What's next for Root

- Work with native speakers, starting with elders, to review every phrase and record it with consent and credit.
- More premium sets, starting with **Talking with elders**: the respectful forms for greeting and visiting family.
- Proverbs and longer spoken stories.
- More languages, led by what learners ask for.
- Publish Root on Google Play.

Because a language survives in the small moments: a greeting at the door, a word at the table, an answer given back in the language it was asked in.

Root is a Next Gen (student) submission for Android.

The code is in our public, MIT-licensed repository: https://github.com/cypher-30/Root

To try it: install the demo APK from our GitHub release, https://github.com/cypher-30/Root/releases/tag/v0.1.0-shipaton (Android 8.0 or later; allow "install unknown apps"; no account). It is a debug-signed demo build with the RevenueCat Test Store connected, so purchases are sandbox and cost nothing. To build it yourself: .\gradlew.bat :app:installDebug. No API key is needed to practise, learn, explore, add your own words or record. The RevenueCat paywall needs a Test Store key in user-level gradle.properties; the README explains this.

Where things stand today:
- RevenueCat is integrated with per-language entitlements (premium_<language>), an all-languages bundle (premium), restore, and an offline entitlement cache.
- Premium is two extra phrase sets per language (US$4.99 one language, US$9.99 all, one-time). They show locked in Explore; "See Premium" opens the paywall, which lists that language's sets.
- Purchases run through RevenueCat's Test Store (sandbox, no real money). Root is not on Google Play yet.
- To open everything without buying, enter the code SHIPATON2026 on the Premium screen (Profile > Root Premium, or See Premium on any locked set). Remove code locks the sets again. The code is local to the device and is not a RevenueCat transaction.
- All phrases, free and premium, are source-checked against public references but not yet reviewed by native speakers, and the app says so. Six phrases play genuine, credited Lingua Libre recordings.

Thank you for looking at Root. We built it for everyone who grew up far from the place their language lives.
