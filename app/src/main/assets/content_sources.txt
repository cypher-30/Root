Amharic / Greetings, Directions
Sixteen phrases (two packs of eight) transliterated from:
FSI Amharic Basic Course, Units 1-2 (Basic Sentences)
Serge Obolensky, Debebow Zelelie, Mulugeta Andualem
Foreign Service Institute, U.S. Department of State, Washington D.C., 1964
This is a work of the United States federal government and carries no
copyright in the U.S. (17 U.S.C. Section 105); it is usable here regardless of
which copy of the scan it was read from. Romanization is exactly as printed
in the 1964 source (its own ad-hoc phonemic scheme, e.g. capital K for a
glottalized/ejective consonant, ñ/š for palatal sounds) -- not Ge'ez script,
and not IPA. This is a small starter selection from the first two of the
course's 50 units, not the full course and not a claim to cover every
register, dialect, or courtesy form.

Status: source-checked against the original 1964 text; native-speaker review
for this app is pending. No audio, illustrations, or other course materials
have been imported -- only these transliterated phrase pairs.
Everything else in the learner's personal Amharic reference collection
(dictionaries, grammars, the Colloquial Amharic course, scripture translations,
etc.) is separately copyrighted and has deliberately not been used as a Root
content source.
